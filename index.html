What works / what doesn't
  • Works: mp4, webm, mkv, mov, mp3, m4a, aac, ogg, wav, vtt, srt and unencrypted HLS (.m3u8).
  • HLS is saved as one .ts file (.mp4 for fMP4 streams). The best quality is picked. Hub 0.9.4+ downloads parts in parallel and decrypts AES-128 streams.
  • DRM and SAMPLE-AES streams are refused by the Hub. Use the Browser tab when you want to pick quality or download only a clip.
  • Progress, cancel and play are on the Hub's Downloads screen (History tab > Downloads screen).
  • If a link says “403” or “HTTP 4xx”, add the page link as referer.
